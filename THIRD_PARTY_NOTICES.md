## ggml and llama.cpp

`GGUF.swift` reads the GGUF container and dequantizes its block formats (Q4_0 to Q8_0, the K-quants, IQ4_NL, IQ4_XS,
MXFP4) to the same values as ggml and its Python reader gguf-py; `GGUFConversion.swift` undoes the tensor transforms of
llama.cpp's converter. `Tests/Fixtures/gguf/dequantize.json` holds gguf-py's output for the check that compares them.
`GGMLMatrixKernels.swift` ports ggml-metal's quantized matrix kernels (`dequantize.h`, `mul_mv.metal`, the simdgroup
`kernel_mul_mm` of `mul_mm.metal`, with `ggml-common.h`'s block layouts and `ggml-metal-impl.h`'s arguments) from
llama.cpp commit `b72050d03`; `GGUFGrids.swift` carries `ggml-common.h`'s IQ codebooks in gguf-py's packed form;
`GGUFQuantize.swift` ports `ggml-quants.c`'s reference quantizers (`make_qkx2_quants`, `quantize_row_q8_0_ref`,
`quantize_row_q2_K_ref`, `quantize_row_q4_K_ref`). `FamilyKernels.swift`, `GLMModel.swift` and `DeepSeekModel.swift`
are written after llama.cpp's glm5-next and deepseek41 graphs, whose numerics they follow.

## Model files in the check fixtures

These fixtures are in the repository for the checks only; the `origami` binary does not contain them.

- `Tests/Fixtures/convert/` holds 16 rows of one Qwen3.8-Flash-Next matrix and the matching rows of its MLX 4-bit
  conversion, so a check can prove bit-exact conversion offline.
- `Tests/Fixtures/models/` holds the models' own `config.json` files and chat templates, copied from their releases
  (and, for the group-32 MLX build, a list of one layer's tensor names and shapes), so checks can read the real
  geometry and render the real templates offline.

Each model's licence applies to its files: Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's Qwen Community License 1.0 (Copyright (c) 2026
Qwen), GLM-5.3-Flash's MIT License (Copyright (c) 2026 Z.AI Co., Ltd) and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash's MIT License
(Copyright (c) 2023 DeepSeek). The MIT terms are those quoted above. The Qwen Community License 1.0 is in the model's
release ([Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next/blob/main/LICENSE)).
